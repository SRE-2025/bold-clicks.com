ChatGPT advertising begins with advertiser eligibility and account readiness.
OpenAI provides Ads Manager in supported countries, including the United States.
Check the current [availability guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability)
for the legal entity that will advertise and be billed.

## Write the brief before building the campaign

Describe the customer problem, the offer and the action the landing page asks
someone to take. Decide what the business would count as a qualified enquiry
or customer. A test without an agreed outcome is difficult to evaluate even
when it produces traffic.

List the materials the campaign would need: approved copy, an appropriate
destination, account permissions, a measurement specification and an agreed
budget. Record who can approve each item. This is a proposed planning
framework, not a claim about a campaign Bold Clicks has already run.

## Confirm the setup path

OpenAI's [account setup guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001213-ads-manager-account-setup)
describes account creation, billing and invitations for other users. An
existing advertiser should use its existing account and grant the relevant
access. Account creation alone does not demonstrate that a campaign is ready
to run.

Do not submit a campaign as a harmless setup exercise. OpenAI says ads created
during onboarding can begin delivering after approval when billing, readiness
and campaign settings allow it. Confirm status and budget before activation.

## Make the launch decision explicit

Keep the creative brief, technical implementation and spending approval as
separate decisions. Document the approved test objective, the reporting
limits and the conditions for pausing or continuing.

The [ChatGPT advertising planning page](/chatgpt-ads/) explains the proposed
scope. [Conversion tracking](/conversion-tracking/) covers the wider
measurement questions. A launch date should follow the readiness checks.
