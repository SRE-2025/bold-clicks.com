Evaluate a ChatGPT advertising test at the traffic, event and business-outcome
levels. Record what each system measures before comparing its totals. A
platform conversion and a customer recorded by the business can describe
different stages of the same journey.

## Specify the business outcome

Write down what counts as a qualified enquiry and who records that decision.
Define the conditions for a booked appointment or a sale. Keep duplicate,
test and unsuccessful submissions out of the outcome used for the business
decision.

Prepare an event specification before implementation: the action, trigger,
destination, expected payload and how a duplicate is identified. Use test
records and the appropriate account permissions to verify delivery. This is
the proposed validation process, not evidence of an integration already
installed for Bold Clicks.

## Check the available measurement route

OpenAI documents a ChatGPT Ads Pixel, Conversions API and selected
[measurement partner integrations](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001416-set-up-measurement-partner-integrations).
The supported capabilities and setup requirements vary by partner. Confirm
the actual account, supported events and implementation requirements rather
than promising that every CRM has a native connection.

Keep secret credentials on the server or in the intended integration's secure
configuration. This editorial draft does not install a tag, change consent or
send customer data to a platform.

## Keep the reporting views distinct

Document the traffic-source tags, event definitions and attribution method.
Compare them with the business's qualified and closed outcomes. Explain
differences instead of treating every total as interchangeable.

Paid ChatGPT traffic and organic referrals need separate source definitions.
Buying a placement is also separate from appearing in an answer: OpenAI's
[consumer ad guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt)
states that ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives.

The useful report ends with a decision: what is supported by the data, what
remains uncertain and what to check next. See [conversion tracking](/conversion-tracking/)
and [ChatGPT planning](/chatgpt-ads/) for the surrounding scope.
