A ChatGPT test budget has distinct parts: media spend, the agency's agreed
work and any measurement or destination work needed to evaluate results.
An ad bid is not a total marketing budget or a promised customer-acquisition
cost.

## Establish the commercial question

Start with the value of the outcome the business wants and the evidence
needed to decide whether to continue. A lead, a qualified enquiry and a
customer are different outcomes. Choose one as the business measure, then
define which earlier events can be measured reliably.

Ask for the media allocation and agency fees to be stated separately. The
proposal should identify setup work, ongoing management, creative revisions
and measurement responsibilities. Bold Clicks' fees and minimums remain
owner inputs; this draft intentionally supplies no price.

## Use current platform settings

OpenAI's [advertiser FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001220-frequently-asked-questions)
describes ChatGPT Ads as a beta whose delivery and spending can vary with the
campaign setup. It also says that broad performance benchmarks across
advertisers and campaign types are not yet established. A claim that every
advertiser should expect the same return would not follow from that guidance.

Confirm the campaign's billing basis, budget settings and limits in the
current account before approving spend. Read the [platform basics](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001207-ads-in-chatgpt-the-basics)
for the available billing and objective options. This guide does not quote a
fixed CPC, CPM or minimum test budget.

## Price the work that makes the test interpretable

List the destination changes, event implementation, reporting work and
creative needed for this offer. Separate reusable setup work from recurring
management. Record what is included, what is excluded and who owns the
accounts and materials.

Agree a review point before launching. The decision to spend more should
follow the business evidence, the available volume and the measurement
limits, rather than an assumed result from another platform.

See [ChatGPT advertising planning](/chatgpt-ads/) and the wider
[paid-media services](/services/) for scope questions.
