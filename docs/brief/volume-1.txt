Instructions for Claude to design and build an elite, conversion-first, 
search-ready website for an ads-only agency.
The job is not to make a generic marketing-agency site. The job is to make Bold Clicks feel like the 
specialized paid-media arm of the Stoneridge family: same DNA, sharper focus, more black, restrained forest
green, and a small amount of gold.
NON-NEGOTIABLE
Use the approved Bold Clicks logo asset exactly as supplied by the owner. Do not redraw it, reinterpret it, or create a 
new logo. The website should make the relationship to Stoneridge visually obvious without looking like a duplicate 
site.
Primary objective
Turn qualified businesses already spending - or ready to spend - on paid advertising into booked audits / strategy calls, while 
building long-term organic authority around PPC and paid-media services.
Research basis: Google Search Central, web.dev, Google Ads Help, W3C/WCAG, Stoneridge Digital, Stoneridge Equity, and current paid-media agency sites. Sources are 
listed at the end of this brief.
DOMAIN STATUS - SEPTEMBER 18, 2026

greenfield build unless the owner provides an existing codebase, DNS/deployment target, or production content that 
must be preserved.

01  /  EXECUTIVE DIRECTION
What Bold Clicks should be
POSITIONING IN ONE SENTENCE
Bold Clicks is the specialist paid-media agency: we run ads, track what happens after the click, and optimize toward 
real business outcomes - not vanity metrics.

This is an ads-only brand. Do not position Bold Clicks as a general SEO, website-design, social-media-management, 
email, reputation, or automation agency.

The narrow specialization is a strength. The site should repeatedly communicate: specialists, not generalists.

The core conversion offer should be a free paid-media / ad-account audit or strategy call. Pick one primary CTA sitewide 
and keep it consistent.

The site must feel premium and serious enough for healthcare, legal, home-services, and established local businesses - 
but fast and direct enough for an operator to understand in seconds.

Every claim must be provable. Never invent ROAS, revenue, client counts, partner badges, case-study numbers, 
testimonials, awards, or platform certifications.
What success looks like
ITEM
REQUIREMENT
Business
Qualified inbound leads and booked calls from businesses with meaningful ad 
budgets.
Brand
Immediate visual connection to Stoneridge, but a distinct paid-media identity.
SEO
Strong crawlability, clear topical architecture, original expert content, and 
indexable pages aligned to real search intent.
CRO

message match between ads and landing pages.
Measurement
Every form, call, booked meeting, qualified lead, and closed customer can be 
attributed back to source/campaign where legally and technically appropriate.
Performance
Fast mobile experience with Core Web Vitals in Google's "good" range on real-
user data wherever feasible.
What current research says
Google continues to emphasize helpful, reliable, people-first content; crawlable links; strong page experience; and technical 
accessibility to Googlebot. Google also says the same core SEO practices apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode - there is no 
separate magic "GEO" shortcut. Current strong paid-media agency sites commonly lead with a concrete outcome, proof, a free 
audit/consultation offer, case studies, service specificity, and measurement language.

Design language

High contrast, spacious, modern, and minimal. More black than Stoneridge Digital, but unmistakably related through green,
cream, and restrained gold.

Flat, crisp brand graphics. Avoid shiny 3D logos, neon gradients, generic stock "marketing" illustrations, rocket ships, fake 
dashboards, and overdone glow effects.

Use subtle ridge / upward-motion geometry as a supporting motif, not as a repeated mountain graphic on every section.

Buttons: primary = forest green or cream-on-black depending on background; secondary = outline. Gold can be a micro-
accent, not a full gold button everywhere.

Motion should be subtle and purposeful: small hover lift, number count-up only if values are real, lightweight reveal 
transitions. No heavy parallax or autoplay background video.

Use authentic team/client/work imagery when available. If no real photography exists, use typography, diagrams, platform 
UI crops, and tasteful abstract brand geometry rather than stock handshake photos.
Voice
VOICE TEST
If the copy could be pasted onto 100 other agencies and still work, rewrite it.

Short, confident, specific sentences. Plain English. Operator-to-operator.

Use the language buyers use: ad spend, qualified leads, booked calls, CAC, cost per lead, revenue, ROAS, tracking, 
landing pages, wasted spend.

Avoid empty phrases such as "unlock your potential," "360-degree marketing," "cutting-edge solutions," "synergy," and 
"results-driven" unless followed by concrete proof.


Build for speed and crawlability first
ITEM
REQUIREMENT
Rendering
Prefer server-rendered or statically generated HTML for core marketing content.

Target good field performance at the 75th percentile: LCP <= 2.5s, INP <= 200ms,

Keep JS lean. Avoid animation libraries or page builders that add large runtime

Use AVIF/WebP where practical; responsive srcset/sizes; explicit width/height; 
lazy-load below-fold images; do not lazy-load the main hero/LCP image.

AI Overviews / AI Mode
Google's 2026 guidance says normal SEO fundamentals remain the foundation for generative AI Search. Build content that is 
crawlable, useful, original, and non-commodity. Claude should optimize for clarity and evidence, not chase gimmicks.

Answer important questions directly near the top, then support them with specifics, examples, and proof.

Use clear entity language: Bold Clicks, what it does, the platforms it manages, industries served, and how its process 
works.

Make original evidence easy to find: case studies, methodology, data, screenshots, named experts, dates.

Keep important content in HTML and crawlable without user interaction.

Do not create unnecessary AI-only files or gimmicks as a substitute for strong content. Google says no new machine-
readable AI text files or special schema are required to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode.

Keyboard-accessible navigation, forms, accordions, dialogs, and menus.

Visible focus states. Do not remove outlines without replacing them with a clearly visible focus treatment.

Text/background contrast that meets accessibility requirements; do not use gold or sage for low-contrast body text.

Comfortable tap targets; WCAG 2.2 adds a minimum target-size criterion of 24x24 CSS px with defined exceptions, but use

Form labels, instructions, accessible error messages, autocomplete attributes, and logical tab order.

Reduced-motion preference respected for animation.

Meaningful alt text; decorative elements excluded from screen-reader noise.

17  /  RESEARCH NOTES + SOURCES
Why these requirements are in the brief
Google Search fundamentals
Google says successful sites should create helpful, reliable, people-first content; use the words people search for in prominent 
places; make links crawlable; and ensure images, video, structured data, and JavaScript are understandable to Search.
Performance
web.dev defines the current Core Web Vitals "good" thresholds as LCP <= 2.5 seconds, INP <= 200 ms, and CLS <= 0.1.
Mobile-first
Google uses the mobile version of content for indexing/ranking and recommends responsive design.
AI Search
Google says the same SEO foundations apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode. There are no extra technical requirements or 
special schema for inclusion; useful, unique content and normal crawlability/indexability fundamentals remain the priority.
Landing-page quality
Google Ads documentation emphasizes relevance, usefulness, ease of navigation, mobile experience, speed, and strong 
message match between ads and landing pages.
Scaled pages
Google's spam policies warn against doorway pages and scaled, low-value content - a reason to avoid mass-generated 
city/industry templates.
Structured data
Google supports Organization and Breadcrumb structured data. FAQ rich results are generally limited to authoritative 
government and health sites.
Measurement
Google Ads and Analytics documentation supports enhanced conversions and lead-funnel events; in 2026 Google expanded 
Data Manager as a key route for enhanced-conversion/offline data.
Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 adds focus and target-size requirements that support keyboard and touch users.
Competitive pattern
Current sites from Disruptive Advertising, HawkSEM, WebFX, and Single Grain consistently use concrete offers, proof/case 
studies, specific paid-media capabilities, strong CTA repetition, and measurement language. Bold Clicks should use the pattern 
without copying their design or copy.
